Salta

Salta is a northern city in Argentina. It shares borders with other provinces such as Jujuy, Catamarca, Tucumán, etc. and with other countries like Chile, Bolivia, and Paraguay. Its shape is like the one of an animal, maybe a fox. It is a touristic city because of all the incredible natural landscapes it has, and that's why it is known as "Salta La Linda" which means "Salta The Beautiful".
